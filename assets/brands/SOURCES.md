# Profile visual assets

These marks identify the institutions, employers, and technologies mentioned in
the owner's resume. All trademarks remain the property of their owners.

- Nanjing University shield: [official university footer SVG](https://www.nju.edu.cn/images/footer_logo.svg).
  Shield view cropped from the university SVG and rendered in NJU purple.
- Sun Yat-sen University seal: [official university identity page](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/xxg/zdjj1.htm),
  [seal PNG](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/__local/C/8E/79/6236A91AEAFBB9F7F2C23A9F836_97F1F3B5_178FF.png).
- Tencent graphic: [official media resources](https://www.tencent.com/newsroom/media-resources/logos/),
  [blue graphic PNG](https://www.tencent.net.cn/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/04_Tencent_Graphics2-2.png).
- ByteDance graphic: inline white logo SVG from the [official website](https://www.bytedance.com/en/),
  with its viewBox restricted to the symbol.
- Technology symbols: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons),
  distributed under CC0; see `CC0-simple-icons.txt`.

The profile uses locally hosted Saira Extra Condensed (500 and 700) and Open Sans
(variable, Latin), matching the typography of the user's reference site. Files
come from Google Fonts; their SIL Open Font License files are in `assets/fonts`.
Chinese text uses the platform's PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei fallback.
